💡 How to solve each problem

For every problem, track these 5 things:

Step	Ask yourself
1️⃣ Understand	What exactly is being asked?
2️⃣ Pattern	Which DSA pattern does this use?
3️⃣ Approach	Can I explain the solution before coding?
4️⃣ Code	Implement without copying
5️⃣ Review	What did I learn / forget?

Don't just count solved problems. The goal is to recognize the pattern behind the problem.
